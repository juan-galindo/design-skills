## What Are Asset Bios?

Asset bios are short descriptions of crypto assets, stocks, and ETFs displayed in the Bitso product. They help users understand what an asset is before buying, selling, or exploring it.

---

## Scope

Applies to:
- Cryptocurrency assets (BTC, ETH, SOL, etc.)
- Stocks (acciones)
- ETFs
- Stablecoins
