# Content Strategy

Source: ZeroHeight — https://bitsodesign.zeroheight.com/styleguide/s/136102/p/8222166

This content strategy document serves as the single source of truth for messaging, tone, and content execution across all touchpoints for a product or feature initiative. It ensures consistency, compliance, and clarity for cross-functional teams (Product, Marketing, Legal, Support).

---

## Naming Convention

Use this format to name your document: **[Initiative name] content strategy**

Example: *Margin Wallet content strategy*

---

## Ownership

| Who | When | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Content Designers** leading product content strategy. **Product Marketing Managers** planning GTM campaigns. **Customer Support** preparing for launch. **Legal/Compliance** reviewing messaging. | For mid-to-large initiatives requiring multi-channel communication. For features with regulatory considerations. When coordinating content across Product, Marketing, and Support. For initiatives requiring localization across markets. | Content Design owns Sections 1–6 (product touchpoints, messaging, terminology). PMM contributes to Section 7 (marketing campaigns & GTM). Legal reviews Section 8 (compliance requirements). All teams align on Section 9 (roadmap & timelines). |
