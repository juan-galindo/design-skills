# Help Center

Source: ZeroHeight — Help center section (pages 8222201, 8222200, 8222199, 8222198, 8222197, 8222196, 8222195)

Help center articles are essential educational, troubleshooting resources that empower users to solve problems independently, understand features, and build confidence in using Bitso.

These articles prioritize clarity, structure, and accessibility while maintaining our **confident yet approachable** and **educational** tone. Help center articles serve to:

- Enable users to self-serve and find answers quickly.
- Build trust through clear, reliable guidance.
- Educate users about complex topics in accessible language.
- Reduce support tickets by addressing common questions proactively.

> New listings articles are not part of the Content team scope.
