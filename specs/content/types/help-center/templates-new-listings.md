## General guidelines

- Create separate tabs for each language, leading with SP-MX first. Use country flags for a more visual scan.
- Use different levels of headings for a proper information architecture.
- Follow the Bitso Style Guide for writing guidelines.
- New listing articles are not owned by the Content team — coordinate with the team responsible before publishing.

---

## Article template

```
[Asset name] is now available on Bitso (Title)

[Intro — Normal text]
Brief description of the asset and why it matters to users.


What is [Asset name]? (H1)
[Body text]
Explain what the asset is, what it does, and its main value proposition.
Keep it factual and neutral — avoid hype language.


How to buy [Asset name] on Bitso (H1)

1. [Step one]: Description.
2. [Step two]: Description.
3. [Step three]: Description.
4. [Closing line]: Confirm the outcome.


Key details (H1)

- Trading pair: [e.g. BTC/MXN]
- Network: [e.g. Ethereum (ERC-20)]
- Deposits: [Available / Not available]
- Withdrawals: [Available / Not available]


Learn more (H1)
[Link to official asset website or whitepaper]
```

## Example

**Title**
Bitcoin Cash (BCH) is now available on Bitso.

**Intro**
You can now buy, sell, and hold Bitcoin Cash (BCH) on Bitso. BCH is a cryptocurrency that emerged from a fork of Bitcoin in 2017, designed for fast and low-cost transactions.

**H1 — What is Bitcoin Cash?**
Bitcoin Cash is a peer-to-peer electronic cash system. It was created to enable faster and cheaper transactions compared to Bitcoin, making it suitable for everyday payments.

**H1 — How to buy Bitcoin Cash on Bitso**
1. Open the Bitso app and go to "Markets".
2. Search for "Bitcoin Cash" or select BCH from the list.
3. Tap "Buy" and enter the amount you want to purchase.
4. Review your order and confirm. Your BCH will appear in your portfolio immediately.

**H1 — Key details**
- Trading pair: BCH/MXN
- Network: Bitcoin Cash
- Deposits: Available
- Withdrawals: Available

**H1 — Learn more**
Visit the official Bitcoin Cash website: https://bitcoincash.org
