# Help Center — Writing Guidelines

---

## Titles

Choose the title type based on whether the user can perform the action:

| Type | When to use | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Direct question** — user's POV | User can perform the action | "How do I buy my first stock?" "How do I increase account limits?" |
| **Indirect statement** — Bitso's POV | Informational, user cannot act | "How market hours work." "How we keep your account safe." |

---

## Capitalization

Use sentence case everywhere — titles, subtitles, bullets, CTAs. Never title case.

- Capitalize proper nouns (Bitso product names, legal documents, people, places).
- Capitalize app section names: go to **Actividad**, not go to **actividad**.
- Use ALL CAPS for crypto tickers and currency codes: BTC, MXN, XRP.
- Lowercase words that follow a number: "2 órdenes nuevas", not "2 Órdenes nuevas".

Full rule: `specs/content/guidelines/capitalization.md`.

---

## Bold

Bold complete scannable ideas — sentences or phrases a reader can understand alone, without surrounding context. Avoid bolding single words in body copy.

**Exception:** you may bold single words when they are labels, CTAs, or app section names.

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| **Abrí tu cuenta en dólares** completando los datos solicitados. | Abrí tu cuenta en **dólares** completando los datos. |
| Tap **Buy** to confirm. | Tap **the buy button** to confirm. |
| Go to **Actividad** to see your history. | Go to **your** activity to see your history. |

Full rule: `specs/content/guidelines/bold.md`.

---

## Bullets

Use bullets whenever information splits into 3+ clear, separate items. Prefer bullets over dense paragraphs.

**Period rule:**
- Add a period when the bullet contains a conjugated verb (complete sentence).
- Skip the period for single words, short phrases, or infinitive-form verbs.

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Envía la invitación. (conjugated verb → period) | Envía la invitación (missing period) |
| **Comprar/Vender:** Acciones principales. (phrase → no period) | **Comprar/Vender:** Acciones principales. (incorrect period on phrase) |

Full rule: `specs/content/guidelines/bullets.md`.

---

## Direct references (UI elements)

Bold section names and CTA labels when referencing them in FAQs.

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Haz clic en **Actividad** | Haz clic en Actividad |
| Haz clic en **Transferir** | Haz clic en Transferir |

> **Note:** This applies to FAQs only. For standalone interface elements, avoid bold.
