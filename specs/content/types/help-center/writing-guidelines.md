# Help Center — Writing Guidelines

---

## Titles

There are 2 options for writing titles and subtitles:

| Indirect question | Direct question |
|---|---|
| Write from the POV of Bitso. | Write from the POV of the user. |
| Use when sharing information users cannot change, actions they cannot perform, or purely informational questions. | Use when users can perform a certain action related to the topic. |
| Examples: "How market hours work." "How we keep your account safe." | Examples: "How do I buy my first stock?" "How do I sell a stock?" "How do I increase account limits?" |

---

## Direct References

Use bold for direct references to section names and actions (CTAs).

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Haz clic en **Actividad** | Haz clic en Actividad |
| | Haz clic en **Transferir** | Haz clic en Transferir |

> **Note:** **Exception** — This only applies to FAQs. For standalone interface elements, always avoid using bold text.
