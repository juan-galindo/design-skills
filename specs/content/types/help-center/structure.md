Every help center article follows a consistent format to make information easy to find. Understanding this structure helps navigate articles quickly and locate exactly what you need.

---

## Key Components

- Clear headings that break content into logical sections
- Step-by-step instructions when applicable
- Visual aids like screenshots or diagrams
- Related article links at the bottom
- A summary or next steps section to wrap up

This standardized approach ensures users spend less time searching and more time solving their issue.

---

## Baseline

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Use either indirect or direct questions depending on content type. Review `help-center-writing-guidelines.md` for title rules. |
| **Introduction** | Provide context in 2-3 sentences. Set clear expectations about what the article covers. |
| **Body** | Use H1 headings (##) for main sections. Structure with numbered lists for step-by-step processes. Use bullet points for non-sequential information. Keep paragraphs short (2-3 sentences maximum). Include visual aids when helpful (screenshots, diagrams). |

---

## Document Format

Create separate tabs for each language, leading with SP-MX first. Use country flags for a more visual scan. Use different levels of headings for a proper information architecture.
