# Help Center — Workflow

Source: ZeroHeight — https://bitsodesign.zeroheight.com/styleguide/s/136102/p/256cfb-workflow

After a feature is designed, these are the steps to create and publish a help center article.

---

| Step | Owner | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | PM | **Gathers all the relevant information** for the creation of the article. |
| 2 | Content designer | Inserts all the information in Claude or other AI tool using a prompt for the **creation of the article in SP-MX**. |
| 3 | Content designer | Uses Claude or other AI tool to **localize content into all language pairs**. |
| 4 | Content designer | Creates a [ticket for the Process Improvement team](https://bitsomx.atlassian.net/jira/core/projects/PROIM/form/38), specifying the section in which the article will be published. |
| 5 | Process Improvement team | Sends the **uploaded article links** in each language. |
| 6 | Content designer | If the article is used within a flow, the article links are connected to Lokalise keys. |
