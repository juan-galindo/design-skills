# Help Center — Self-Checklist Before Publishing

- [ ] Is the language clear and free of unnecessary jargon?
- [ ] Can users easily scan and find what they need?
- [ ] Have I anticipated and addressed potential concerns?
- [ ] Are instructions specific and actionable?
- [ ] Would users trust and feel supported by this content?
- [ ] Am I informing without overwhelming?
- [ ] Have I used proper formatting (bullets, headings, bold)?
