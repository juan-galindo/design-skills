# Help Center — Content Strategy

Help center content should be rooted in the **confident yet approachable** pillar, providing clear, reliable solutions with empathy. The **educational** element is crucial for guiding users through processes and building understanding.

---

## Main Considerations

- Lead with the most important information.
- Break complex topics into digestible steps.
- Use plain language, avoiding unnecessary jargon.
- When technical terms are necessary, explain them clearly.
- Anticipate user questions and address concerns proactively.

---

## Four Pillars

| Clarity | Empathy | Scannability | Consistency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Explain complex topics with assured simplicity. Never assume something is self-explanatory. Define technical terms when first introduced. Use concrete examples when possible. | Acknowledge potential concerns or confusion. Provide reassurance backed by facts. Anticipate follow-up questions. Offer multiple paths to success when available. | Use descriptive headings that clearly indicate content. Break up long text with subheadings. Prioritize bullet points and numbered lists over dense paragraphs. Make key information visually distinct. | Use standard terminology from the glossary. Follow regional language conventions (SP-MX, SP-AR, SP-CO, PT-BR, EN). Maintain consistent capitalization (sentence case). Apply active voice consistently. |

---

## Tone by Article Type

| Article type | Suggested tone |
|---|---|
| Technical/Process | **Primary**: Confident yet approachable. **Secondary**: Educational. Keep instructions clear and actionable. Use active voice from the user's perspective. Example: "Andá a tu **Perfil** en la esquina superior izquierda" |
| Security | **Primary**: Confident yet approachable with emphasis on reassurance. **Secondary**: Educational. Address concerns directly and empathetically. Provide clear rationale for security measures. Example: "Esta validación es válida por 10 minutos." |
| Feature explanations | **Primary**: Educational + confident. **Secondary**: Inspiring (when appropriate). Explain benefits clearly. Use relatable analogies for complex concepts. Connect features to user goals. |
