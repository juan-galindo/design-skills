# Voice & Tone

Source: ZeroHeight — https://bitsodesign.zeroheight.com/styleguide/s/136102/p/84fea8-voice-and-tone + [Tone of voice guidelines (Google Slides)](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1Cyvq3vNVmhSPf3090zgARSozCQ5C0CK47sdtX3zTS7s/edit?slide=id.p#slide=id.p)

At Bitso, every word matters. The way we communicate shapes how people experience our platform and how confident they feel making financial decisions. To communicate effectively, we rely on 2 essential elements: **voice** and **tone**.

> **Voice** is who we are: our personality and core values that remain constant across every interaction. It's the consistent thread that makes Bitso recognizable and trustworthy, whether someone is reading an email, navigating the app, or learning about crypto for the first time.
>
> **Tone** is how we adapt that voice to different situations. Just like you'd speak differently when helping someone through a mistake versus celebrating their first investment, our tone shifts to match the context while staying true to who we are.

**Voice** sets our foundation through 3 core pillars that define our personality and remain consistent across all touchpoints.
**Tone** brings flexibility through the FEEL framework: a practical system that balances function, emotion, education, and learning. See `core/feel-framework.md`.

---

## Introduction

Bitso's voice blends deep expertise, human connection, and bold creativity — designed to make people feel empowered, motivated, and seen.

The three core pillars are:
1. Confident yet Approachable
2. Inspiring but Down to Earth
3. Fresh with Purpose

Not every message needs to emphasize all three pillars equally. Flex thoughtfully based on the situation, always staying true to who Bitso is: credible, motivating, and refreshingly human.

## Pillar 1: Confident yet Approachable

### What It Means
Bitso speaks with clarity and confidence grounded in deep knowledge — but never flexes for the sake of it. Authority comes from experience, not ego. The goal is to inform, empower, and guide — never to talk down, gatekeep, or overwhelm. This tone earns trust by being credible and clear while always staying human and relatable.

### What This Sounds Like
- **Clear, calm, and confident:** Direct language that projects assurance without overwhelming.
- **Professional, but never stiff:** Refined tone — clear and confident, never cold or corporate.
- **Direct, but empathetic:** Gets to the point while acknowledging user needs and concerns.
- **Thoughtful, not wordy:** Words chosen with care; no unnecessary filler.
- **Confident, not arrogant:** Projects expertise without belittling or alienating the reader.
- **Purposefully educational:** Where technical terms are essential, introduce and clearly explain them — transforming complexity into empowerment.

> "We write like someone who's been here before and knows what they're doing — but still knows how to listen."

### Dos
- Explain complex topics with assured simplicity: "Here's what that means — and why it matters."
- Be empathetic when it matters. Acknowledge concerns and offer reassurance backed by facts.
- Use technical terms only when helpful — and explain them in plain language.
- Anticipate and address potential doubts with empathy.

### Don'ts
- Don't assume something is self-explanatory.
- Don't rely on self-praise to build trust. Show it through clear facts and a confident, yet humble, tone.
- Don't use jargon or buzzwords to sound impressive.
- Don't shut down disagreement or assume the reader knows less.

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- **Overcorrecting into coldness:** Confidence shouldn't feel robotic or overly formal.
- **Defaulting to complex language:** Authority comes from clarity, not complexity.
- **Coming across as preachy:** Bitso is here to support, inform, and empower — not lecture.

### When to Dial Up or Down
- **Dial up:** Major product/feature launches, terms of service/policy updates, protocol upgrades or network migrations.
- **Dial down:** Social media posts, onboarding tutorials, push notifications and in-app messages.

### Examples (Español)
| Antes | Después |
|---|---|
| Nuestra plataforma agrega datos de mercado en tiempo real para cripto y acciones. | Obtén información del mercado en tiempo real sobre cripto y acciones. Así puedes invertir con confianza y siempre ir un paso adelante. |
| Para continuar, el usuario debe completar el Nivel 2 de KYC. | Para acceder a límites más altos, solo necesitas completar algunos pasos de verificación adicionales. Te acompañamos en el proceso. |
| Las solicitudes de retiro se procesan según nuestros protocolos internos de seguridad. | Tu retiro se está procesando de forma segura. Nuestras medidas de protección te respaldan. |
| Debido a la saturación de la red, tu transacción podría experimentar demoras. | Nuestra red está ocupada en este momento, lo que podría causar una pequeña demora. Continuamos monitoreando para que tu transacción se procese sin problemas. |

### Examples (English)
| Before | After |
|---|---|
| To proceed, user must complete KYC Level 2. | To unlock higher limits, simply complete a few more verification steps. We'll guide you. |
| Our platform aggregates real-time market data for crypto and stocks. | Get real-time market insights on crypto and stocks — so you can invest confidently and stay a step ahead. |
| Withdrawal requests are processed per our internal security protocols. | Your withdrawal is being processed safely, backed by our security measures. |
| Due to network congestion, your transaction may experience latency. | The network is currently busy, which might cause a slight delay. We're monitoring it to ensure your transaction goes through smoothly. |

### Quick Self-Checklist
- [ ] Is the language clear and free of unnecessary jargon?
- [ ] Does it sound like a knowledgeable guide, not a lecturer?
- [ ] Would users trust and feel comfortable talking to us?
- [ ] Are we informing without overwhelming?

## Pillar 2: Inspiring but Down to Earth

### What It Means
Bitso drives change with transparency — not hype or empty dreams. The words energize and uplift without slipping into fluff or false promises. Optimistic, not idealistic. This tone is about offering hope — with substance.

### What This Sounds Like
- **Uplifting, but grounded:** Positive visions always tied back to real actions or outcomes.
- **Optimistic, not overpromising:** Believes in the future but avoids exaggerations or guarantees.
- **Clear-eyed, not naïve:** Recognizes challenges while encouraging perseverance and growth.
- **Warm and inclusive:** Invites everyone into the journey, not just a select few.
- **Purposeful and forward-looking:** Every message connects today's actions to a better tomorrow.

> "We're the quiet spark — not the megaphone. The kind of voice that earns belief, not just attention."

### Dos
- Paint a tangible, hopeful vision: "Together, we can build something better."
- Use real-world examples to inspire.
- Acknowledge challenges — but highlight what's possible.

### Don'ts
- Don't promise vague, unrealistic outcomes: "The future is limitless!"
- Don't rely on vague, lofty language.
- Don't pretend everything's perfect or effortless.

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- **Overhyping:** Inspiration loses power when it feels exaggerated. Users become skeptical instead of hopeful.
- **Using clichés:** Generic phrases like "change the world" sound hollow without real context or proof.
- **Neglecting the present:** Dreaming big must always be grounded in today's reality to feel attainable.

### When to Dial Up or Down
- **Dial up:** Vision or mission statements, brand campaigns, founder letters or manifesto-style messaging.
- **Dial down:** Product specifications, technical documentation, operational updates.

### Examples (Español)
| Antes | Después |
|---|---|
| Nuestra plataforma ofrece un entorno seguro y versátil para el comercio de activos digitales. | Imagina un futuro financiero con más posibilidades. Bitso pone en tus manos las herramientas para construirlo, de forma simple y segura. |
| Utiliza nuestra función de ahorros para generar ingresos pasivos con tus fondos. | Tu cripto hace más que solo estar esperando. Gana rendimientos con tus fondos y mira crecer tu portafolio cada semana. |
| Ofrecemos opciones de inversión diversificadas para hacer crecer tu portafolio. | Diversifica tu portafolio con opciones menos predecibles y descubre nuevas formas de hacer crecer tu inversión. |
| Únete a nuestra comunidad para acceder a servicios cripto. | Aquí eres parte de algo más grande. Forma parte de la economía digital junto a millones de personas que están transformando su vida, una transacción a la vez. |

### Examples (English)
| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Our platform provides users with a secure and versatile environment for trading digital assets. | Imagine a financial future with more possibilities. Bitso puts the tools in your hands to build it, simply and securely. |
| Utilize our savings feature to earn passive income on your holdings. | Your crypto, doing more than just sitting there. Earn yields on your holdings and watch your portfolio grow, week after week. |
| We offer diverse investment options for portfolio growth. | Diversify your portfolio beyond the obvious and discover new ways to build your wealth. |
| Join our community to access crypto services. | You're part of something bigger here. Step into the digital economy alongside millions who are shaping their financial lives, one transaction at a time. |

### Quick Self-Checklist
- [ ] Does the optimism feel earned, not exaggerated?
- [ ] Are we connecting today's actions to a bigger vision?
- [ ] Have we avoided vague promises and focused on tangible outcomes?
- [ ] Would a reader feel motivated *and* respected?

## Pillar 3: Fresh with Purpose

### What It Means
Fresh means standing out for the right reasons. Bitso's voice is creative, unexpected, and expressive — but not just to be different. It brings new perspectives and energy that make people pause and pay attention, while staying clear and on-point. Bold in style, but never random.

### What This Sounds Like
- **Original, authentic:** Uses its own voice — doesn't copy trends.
- **Playful, not gimmicky:** Humor and style serve a purpose.
- **Energetic, not loud:** Brings momentum and vibrancy without shouting at the reader.
- **Creative, but grounded in purpose:** Every surprising element drives clarity or emotion.
- **Human and relatable:** Speaks like real people, not brands trying too hard to be "fun."

> "We speak with personality, and we're not afraid to break convention — but only when it adds clarity, emotion, or meaning."

### Dos
- Challenge norms constructively: "Rethink what money can do. Bitso shows you how."
- Demystify complexity with ingenuity: "We make finance finally make sense."
- Speak with thoughtful originality.
- Infuse energy while maintaining focus.
- Use metaphors or surprising phrasing to simplify and engage: "Turn market noise into smart moves."

### Don'ts
- Don't dismiss existing systems disrespectfully: "Let's skip the boring stuff—here's the good part."
- Don't use hype without substance: " Let's GO!! (Meaningless emojis and uninformative exclamations)."
- Don't be clever just for the sake of it, losing the message.
- Don't sacrifice clarity for quirkiness: "Our app's so lit, it's basically financial fireworks."
- Don't adopt fleeting trends or slang for attention: "It's giving 'investor goals' — you know?"

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- **Trying too hard to be different:** Forced originality feels awkward and damages credibility.
- **Overusing informal tricks:** Emojis, slang, or edgy phrasing can backfire if they don't match the moment or audience.
- **Prioritizing style over substance:** If creativity clouds the meaning, user trust and attention are lost.

### When to Dial Up or Down
- **Dial up:** Campaigns and ads, social captions, brand videos and storytelling.
- **Dial down:** Serious topics, crisis communications, formal documents or contracts.

### Examples (Español)
| Antes | Después |
|---|---|
| Hemos actualizado nuestro proceso de registro para mejorar la experiencia del usuario. | Una bienvenida que por fin tiene sentido. Pasos más rápidos, tips más claros… y sí, terminarás de registrarte antes de que se enfríe tu café. |
| Nuestra nueva función permite transferencias cripto optimizadas. | Enviar cripto ahora es más fluido. Menos fricción, más flow, porque tu tiempo vale cripto. |
| Mantente informado con actualizaciones del mercado. | Dile adiós a los datos vacíos. Obtén aprendizajes que te ayuden a tomar decisiones inteligentes. |
| Aprende sobre la tecnología blockchain. | Vamos más allá de seguir las modas: aquí hacemos que el cripto por fin tenga sentido. |

### Examples (English)
| Before | After |
|---|---|
| We've updated our onboarding process for a better user experience. | Say hello to onboarding that actually makes sense. Faster steps, clearer tips — and yes, you'll be done before your coffee gets cold. |
| Our new feature allows for optimized crypto transfers. | Sending crypto just got smoother. Less friction, more flow — because your time is crypto. |
| Stay informed with market updates. | Get insights that help you make smart moves, not just more data. |
| Learn about blockchain technology. | Beyond the buzzwords: we make crypto finally make sense. |

### Quick Self-Checklist
- [ ] Is the creativity serving clarity or emotion — not just novelty?
- [ ] Are we using original language instead of chasing trends?
- [ ] Would a real person find this surprising *and* understandable?
- [ ] Is the energy adding value, not just noise?

## Balancing the Pillars

1. **Start with confidence:** Establish trust by sounding knowledgeable and clear.
2. **Layer in inspiration:** Once trust is built, motivate users by connecting actions to meaningful outcomes.
3. **Add freshness where it matters:** Bring energy, originality, and surprise to moments where attention is key — always in service of clarity and connection.

## Content Principles

For content design, our tone of voice isn't just about words — it's about **shaping the entire user experience** and building trust, sparking momentum, and leaving a lasting impression.

### 1. Start with user needs and context (the "why")

- **Understand the user's state:** Before writing, know their mindset (confused, excited, frustrated?). This guides your tone choice.
- **Prioritize tone by need:**
- **Confident yet approachable:** For trust-building moments (onboarding, security, finance).
- **Inspiring but down-to-earth:** For motivating action, calls to action, or new possibilities. Keep it real.
- **Fresh with purpose:** For capturing attention, delight, or differentiation (if it adds value, not just noise).

### 2. Translate principles into design decisions (the "how")

| | |
|---|---|
| **Microcopy matters** | Every word, from buttons to error messages, reflects our tone. **Confident:** "Confirm your transaction." (Clear, direct). **Empathetic:** "Something went wrong. Please try again." (Guides to action). **Uplifting:** "Unlock new possibilities." (Motivating). |
| **Information hierarchy & structure** | **Confident:** Use clear headings, bullets, and concise sentences. Clarity is authority. **Inspiring:** Break down complexity; make goals feel attainable. |
| **Visuals & interaction align** | Ensure imagery and animations enhance the chosen tone (e.g., "Confident" visuals aren't overly formal or too casual). "Fresh" interactions should genuinely delight, not just be gimmicky. |
| **Handle error states & support with care** | **Confident & empathetic:** Clearly explain issues and next steps. "We're experiencing high volume, please try again in a few minutes." **Inspiring & grounded:** Offer reassurance; guide users toward solutions. "We're working hard to get you back on track so you can continue your crypto journey." |

### 3. Test & iterate

- **Don't assume:** Test content with real users to check if the intended tone is received as planned.
- **Refine:** Use feedback to continuously improve language and design, ensuring consistent alignment with the Bitso voice.

> Every word in content design is an opportunity to strengthen our relationship with our users.

## Mexican-Spanish First (SP-MX)

Given that Mexico represents Bitso's largest user base, tailoring the user experience to their linguistic and cultural preferences is crucial for maintaining user satisfaction, trust, and driving further adoption.

| Dominant user base | Enhanced UX | Brand consistency |
|---|---|---|
| Mexico has the strongest brand recognition and trust, established with over **8 million users**. Designing primarily in Mexican Spanish directly caters to the language of the majority of our users, fostering a more intuitive and comfortable experience. | **Clarity and comprehension:** Using the specific vocabulary, idioms, and linguistic nuances of Mexican Spanish minimizes ambiguity and ensures accurate interpretation of information, reducing user errors and frustration. **Accessibility:** Providing content in their native language demonstrates inclusivity and makes the platform more accessible to a broader range of users within Mexico, including those with varying levels of digital literacy. **Cultural relevance:** Adapting the language to reflect Mexican cultural references and linguistic styles creates a sense of familiarity and connection, strengthening the user's emotional engagement with the Bitso brand. | **Reinforcing trust and familiarity:** Maintaining consistency with the language used in marketing, customer support, and other touchpoints reinforces Bitso's strong brand presence in Mexico and builds upon the existing trust and familiarity. **Authenticity:** Using authentic Mexican Spanish demonstrates that Bitso understands and values its Mexican users, fostering a stronger sense of loyalty. |
