# Lateral Bar (/)

The lateral bar (/) joins two related words or options into a single compact element. Use it when space is limited or when two terms are closely linked, like actions, alternatives, or inclusive language forms.

---

Use the lateral bar **with no spaces** between the words it separates.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Comprar/Vender | Comprar / Vender |
| | Compra acciones 24/7 | Compra acciones 24 / 7 |

---

**Capitalize the first letter of each word** when the bar separates distinct actions (like CTAs) or categories that stand on their own. When the words are part of a running sentence, keep them lowercase.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Comprar/Vender (CTA) | Comprar/vender (CTA) |
| | Puedes comprar cripto/acciones/ETFs | Puedes comprar Cripto/Acciones/ETFs |

---

**Use lowercase** when the 2 elements form a single unit, like gender-inclusive forms.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Beneficiario/a | Beneficiario/A |

---

**Accessibility warning**

Avoid lateral bars whenever possible and only use them as a last resort due to space constraints, as screen readers may not pause appropriately between words, creating confusion for users with visual impairments.
