# Capitalization

Source: ZeroHeight — https://bitsodesign.zeroheight.com/styleguide/s/136102/p/8222179-capitalization

Capitalization shapes how approachable and readable content feels. Inconsistent casing creates visual noise and makes text harder to scan, especially on mobile. Bitso follows sentence case across all surfaces to keep things clear, consistent, and human.

---

**Use sentence case** for all content — body copy, titles, subtitles, links, and CTAs. We don't use title case. Ever.

> **Note:** Sentence case means that only the first letter of the first word is a capital. Like this sentence.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Do: Donde tu dinero viene a crecer | Don't: Donde Tu Dinero Viene a Crecer |

**Rationale**
- Easier to read on digital screens and mobile devices
- More conversational — matches Bitso's approachable tone
- Less cognitive load for users
- Modern standard used by most apps and websites today
- Better for localization across different languages

---

**Capitalize the first letter of every word** (except prepositions and conjunctions) when referring to **proper nouns** — the name of a person, a Bitso product, a legal document, or a place.

> **Note:** About prepositions and conjunctions: prepositions show relationships (in, on, at, to, from, with, for, of, about). Conjunctions connect words or ideas (and, but, or, so, yet, because, if, when).

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Do: Stablecoin Conference LATAM | Don't: Stablecoin conference LATAM |
| | Do: Términos y condiciones | Don't: Términos y Condiciones |
| | Do: Aviso de privacidad | Don't: Aviso de Privacidad |
| | Do: Chatea con Soporte | Don't: Chatea con soporte |

> **Note:** **Exception**: Don't capitalize generic crypto technical terms like blockchain, network, market cap.

---

**Capitalize the first letter of the first word** when referring to section names in the app or website.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Do: Ir a Actividad | Don't: Ir a actividad |
| | Do: Regresar a Inicio | Don't: Volver al inicio |

---

**Use all caps** when referring to crypto tickers, currency codes, or stock symbols.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Do: Tienes un saldo de 1,200 MXN | Don't: Tienes un saldo de 1,200 mxn |
| | Do: Puedes ganar hasta 1.5% anual en XRP | Don't: Puedes ganar hasta 1.5% anual en Xrp |

> **Warning:** **Warning**: Avoid ALL-CAPS in all cases except these for accessibility reasons — text is harder to scan. Exception: website preheaders written in small fonts.

---

**Use lowercase** for words that come right after a number. Treat the word after a number as you would any other word mid-sentence.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Do: Cantidad: 2 órdenes nuevas | Don't: Cantidad: 2 Órdenes nuevas |
