Punctuation shapes how users read, pause, and feel about our content. When used consistently, it builds trust and makes the interface easier to scan.

---

## Colon :

Use a colon to introduce a list, an explanation, or a direct continuation of an idea.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Do: Estas son las acciones principales: AAPL. MSFT. AMZN. | Don't: Estas son las acciones principales. AAPL. MSFT. AMZN. |

---

Use sentence case after a colon when what follows is a full sentence.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Do: Buenas noticias: Tu depósito se realizó. | Don't: Buenas noticias: tu depósito se realizó. |

---

Use lowercase after a colon when what follows is a fragment, a list item, or a label.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Do: Comprar/Vender: acciones principales de acceso rápido | Don't: Comprar/Vender: Acciones principales de acceso rápido |
| | Do: Estado: pendiente | Don't: Estado: Pendiente |

---

## Comma ,

Use a comma to separate a person's name from the rest of the sentence when addressing them directly (vocative comma).

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Do: Emilia, tu cuenta está lista | Don't: Emilia tu cuenta está lista |
| | Do: Tu cuenta está lista, Emilia | Don't: Tu cuenta está lista Emilia |

---

Avoid the Oxford comma (serial comma) before the last item in a list.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| EN | Do: Crypto, stocks and cash | Don't: Crypto, stocks, and cash |

---

## Dashes

### Em dash (—)

Avoid em dashes whenever possible. Use a period, comma, or rewrite the sentence instead.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Do: Make sure your connection is working. | Don't: Make sure your connection is working—try again. |

> **Note:** **Exception** — Em dashes can be used sparingly in marketing or campaign copy when they add rhythm and clarity.

---

### En dash (–)

Use the en dash to indicate a range of numbers, dates, or times. No spaces on either side.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Do: 9:00 am–5:00 pm | Don't: 9:00 am - 5:00 pm |
| | Do: 2020–2025 | Don't: 2020 - 2025 |

---

## Ellipsis …

Use the ellipsis character (…), not three separate periods (...).

Use ellipses only to indicate a loading state or truncated text in the UI. Avoid them in body copy, titles, or CTAs.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Do: Cargando… (loading state) | Don't: Verifica tu correo… |
| | Do: Verifica tu correo para continuar. | |

---

## Exclamation/Question marks ¡! ¿?

In Spanish, use opening and closing marks. In Portuguese and English, use only the closing mark.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| SP | Do: ¡Hola, Sergio! ¿Olvidaste tu contraseña? | Don't: Hola, Sergio! Olvidaste tu contraseña? |
| PT-BR | Do: Oi, Sergio! Esqueceu sua senha? | Don't: ¡Oi, Sergio! ¿Esqueceu sua senha? |
| EN | Do: Hi, Sergio! Forgot your password? | Don't: ¡Hi, Sergio! ¿Forgot your password? |

---

Use exclamation marks sparingly. Reserve them for genuine moments of celebration or delight, not as decoration.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Do: ¡Activaste Rendimientos! | Don't: ¡Deposita hoy! ¡No esperes! ¡Es fácil! |

---

Never use more than one exclamation or question mark in a row.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Do: ¡Lo lograste! | Don't: ¡¡Lo lograste!! |

---

## Hyphen -

Use hyphens to join compound modifiers before a noun.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| EN | Do: real-time data | Don't: real time data |
| | Do: step-by-step process | Don't: step by step process |

---

## Parenthesis ()

Use parentheses to indicate optional fields in forms. Write the label in lowercase.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Do: Apellido materno (opcional) | Don't: Apellido materno (Opcional) |

---

Punctuation that belongs to the full sentence goes outside the closing parenthesis. Punctuation that belongs only to the parenthetical content goes inside.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Do: Revisa tu saldo (se actualiza todos los días). | Don't: Revisa tu saldo (se actualiza todos los días.) |
| | | Don't: Revisa tu saldo. (Se actualiza todos los días.) |

---

Use parentheses to introduce an acronym after its full name on first mention. Review `guidelines/acronyms.md`.

---

## Period .

Titles and subtitles don't take periods.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Do: Tu portafolio | Don't: Tu portafolio. |

---

Email subject lines don't take periods.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Do: Sergio, ya puedes comprar acciones | Don't: Sergio, ya puedes comprar acciones. |

---

CTAs don't take periods or other punctuation marks.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Do: Explorar acciones | Don't: ¡Explorar acciones! |

---

## Semicolon ;

Avoid semicolons in UI copy. Split the sentence into 2 instead.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Do: Verifica tu correo. Puede tardar unos minutos. | Don't: Verifica tu correo; puede tardar unos minutos. |

> **Note:** **Exception** — In long-form content (like help center articles), semicolons can connect 2 closely related independent clauses. Use them sparingly.

---

## Slash /

Use the lateral bar **with no spaces** between the words it separates.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Do: Comprar/Vender | Don't: Comprar / Vender |
| | Do: Compra acciones 24/7 | Don't: Compra acciones 24 / 7 |

---

**Capitalize the first letter of each word** when the bar separates distinct actions (like CTAs) or categories that stand on their own. Keep them lowercase when they are part of a running sentence.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Do: Comprar/Vender (CTA) | Don't: Comprar/vender (CTA) |
| | Do: Puedes comprar cripto/acciones/ETFs | Don't: Puedes comprar Cripto/Acciones/ETFs |

---

**Use lowercase** when the 2 elements form a single unit, like gender-inclusive forms.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Do: Beneficiario/a | Don't: Beneficiario/A |

> **Accessibility note —** **Accessibility warning** — Avoid lateral bars whenever possible and only use them as a last resort due to space constraints, as screen readers may not pause appropriately between words, creating confusion for users with visual impairments.
