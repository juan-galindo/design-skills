Symbols are characters that represent words, concepts, or values in a shorter form. They help save space and improve scannability, but only when used consistently.

---

## & — Ampersand

The ampersand (&) is a symbol used in place of the word "and."

Avoid the use of the ampersand symbol (&) whenever possible.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Do: Términos y condiciones | Don't: Términos & condiciones |

> **Accessibility note —** **Accessibility warning** — Screen readers may read "&" inconsistently across languages and devices. Use "and" (or its equivalent) to ensure a smoother reading experience for users with visual impairments.

> **Note:** **Exception** — Keep the ampersand symbol (&) when space is severely limited.

## % — Percentage

Use the percentage symbol (%) attached to the number, with no space between them.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Do: Tu saldo creció 10% este mes | Don't: Tu saldo creció 10 % este mes |

---

Use numerals instead of writing out the word "percent."

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Do: Tu saldo creció 10% este mes | Don't: Tu saldo creció diez por ciento este mes |
