# Active voice

Source: ZeroHeight — https://bitsodesign.zeroheight.com/styleguide/s/136102/p/20f976-active-voice

Active voice puts the subject first and makes clear who is doing the action. It's more direct, easier to read, and reflects Bitso's confident yet approachable tone.

---

**Use the active voice** instead of the passive voice, either from the user's or Bitso's POV.

## User's POV

The user is the subject performing the action.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Do: Actualizaste tu dirección de Ethereum | Don't: Tu dirección de Ethereum fue actualizada |

## Bitso's POV

When Bitso is the subject performing the action, **use "we"** instead of the brand name. This keeps the tone human and conversational, rather than distant or corporate.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| SP-MX SP-AR SP-CO | Do: Estamos procesando tu transacción | Don't: Tu transacción está siendo procesada |
| | | Don't: Bitso está procesando tu transacción |
| EN | Do: We're processing your request | Don't: Your request is being processed |
| | | Don't: Bitso is processing your request |
| PT-BR | Do: Estamos processando sua transação | Don't: Sua transação está sendo processada |
| | | Don't: Bitso está processando sua transação |

**Rationale**

- **Direct and concise:** uses fewer words and eliminates unnecessary helping verbs.
- **Clear accountability:** users know exactly who is responsible for each action in their financial transactions.
- **Reliability:** direct ownership of actions feels more reliable.
- **Confident tone:** matches Bitso's "Confident yet Approachable" voice by taking clear responsibility rather than hiding behind passive language.
