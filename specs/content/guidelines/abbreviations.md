# Abbreviations

Source: ZeroHeight — https://bitsodesign.zeroheight.com/styleguide/s/136102/p/274704-abbreviations

Abbreviations are shortened forms of words, usually created by cutting the word and adding a period.

---

**Use sentence case** for abbreviations and add a period at the end.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Do: approx. | Don't: approx |
| | Do: etc. | Don't: etc |
| | Do: max. | Don't: max |

---

> **Accessibility note —** **Accessibility warning**

Avoid abbreviations whenever possible unless space is severely limited (for example, in tables or labels), as screen readers may not read them correctly.
