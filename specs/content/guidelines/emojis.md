# Emojis

Source: ZeroHeight — https://bitsodesign.zeroheight.com/styleguide/s/136102/p/18d144-emojis

Emojis are for marketing content only. Do not use them in the product UI, including buttons, error messages, labels, notifications, or any in-app copy.

---

Use emojis as a visual complement, never as a replacement for words.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | ✅ Tu tarjeta está en camino 🛻 | ❌ Tu tarjeta está en 🛻 |

---

Use no more than 1 emoji at a time.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | ✅ Tu tarjeta está en camino 🛻 | ❌ Tu tarjeta está en camino 🛻🪪 |

---

When an emoji follows a sentence, place it after the period, not before.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | ✅ Tu tarjeta está en camino. 🛻 | ❌ Tu tarjeta está en camino 🛻. |

> **Accessibility note —** **Accessibility warning** — Place emojis at the beginning or end of a sentence, never in the middle. Screen readers read them aloud, which disrupts the reading flow for people using assistive technology.
