# Acronyms

Source: ZeroHeight — https://bitsodesign.zeroheight.com/styleguide/s/136102/p/5037c7-acronyms

Acronyms are formed with the first letter of each word and written in all caps, with no periods or spaces (e.g., FAQ, ATM, CEO).

---

Acronyms are written in all caps, with no periods or spaces.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Do: LATAM | Don't: Latam · Don't: L.A.T.A.M. |
| | Do: CEO | Don't: C.E.O. |
| | Do: FAQ | Don't: F.A.Q. |
| | Do: ATM | Don't: A.T.M. |

---

The plural version of acronyms takes a lowercase "s" at the end.

| Region | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| All | Do: FAQs | Don't: FAQS · Don't: FAQ's |

> **Note:** While Spanish and Portuguese grammar traditionally don't pluralize acronyms this way, we follow this convention because it's the most widely used form online.

---

For acronyms referring to financial institutions or complex financial terms, follow this suggestion:

- **First-time mention:** Write out the full name, then show the acronym in parentheses.
- **Second-time mention:** Use the acronym alone.

| Region | Do |
|---|---|
| SP-MX | Do: La Comisión Nacional Bancaria y de Valores (CNBV) supervisa las instituciones financieras en México. La CNBV asegura el cumplimiento de los estándares regulatorios. |
| EN | Do: The Financial Industry Regulatory Authority (FINRA) regulates brokerage firms and exchange markets. FINRA protects investors by ensuring the securities industry operates fairly. |

---

When using an acronym in a different language, translate the full name but keep the original acronym. You can add the country it belongs to for full context.

| Region | Do |
|---|---|
| SP-MX | Do: La Autoridad Reguladora de la Industria Financiera (FINRA) regula las firmas de corretaje en Estados Unidos. FINRA protege a los inversionistas. |
| | Do: La Autoridad Reguladora de la Industria Financiera de Estados Unidos (FINRA) regula las firmas de corretaje. FINRA protege a los inversionistas. |
| EN | Do: The National Banking and Securities Commission (CNBV) oversees financial institutions in Mexico. The CNBV ensures compliance with regulatory standards. |
| | Do: Mexico's National Banking and Securities Commission (CNBV) oversees financial institutions in Mexico. The CNBV ensures compliance with regulatory standards. |
