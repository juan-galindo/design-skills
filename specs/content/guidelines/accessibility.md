# Accessibility

Source: ZeroHeight — https://bitsodesign.zeroheight.com/styleguide/s/136102/p/52daa4-accessibility

Good content is content everyone can use. These guidelines help make Bitso's interface clearer, more inclusive, and easier to navigate for people with different abilities and needs.

---

Accessible content removes barriers. It benefits not just users with disabilities, but everyone who interacts with Bitso.

**Keep these principles in mind:**

- Write for clarity first. Clear content is accessible content.
- Avoid language that assumes a specific ability, like sight or hearing.
- Use inclusive language that doesn't exclude or label people.
- Test your content with accessibility tools when possible.
