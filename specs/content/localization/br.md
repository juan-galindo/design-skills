# Locale: PT-BR — Brazilian Portuguese

Sources: glossary.md, feel-framework.md, cta-guidelines.md, help-center.md

---

## Register

- **Pronoun**: "você"
- **Tone**: Friendly but slightly more formal than es dialects. Contractions optional but natural.
- **Key rule**: This is a full language adaptation — not just vocabulary swaps. Natural Brazilian Portuguese flow is required.

---

## Verb Conjugations

| Form | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| CTAs / buttons | **Infinitive** — same as es | "Comprar", "Enviar", "Continuar" / "Compre", "Envie" |
| Body instructions | Você imperative | "Vá para seu Perfil", "Clique em Atividade" |
| Educational content | Rhetorical questions | "Sabia que você pode programar compras?" |
| Success states | Warm but understated | "Pronto! Você comprou…" |

### Help Center Examples
- SP-MX "Ve a tu Perfil" → PT-BR "**Vá** para seu Perfil"
- SP-MX "Puedes aumentar tus límites" → PT-BR "Você **pode** aumentar seus limites"
- SP-MX "Haz clic en Actividad" → PT-BR "**Clique** em Atividade"

## Error Messages

- Shared accountability: **"Tivemos um problema"**
- Apology: **"Desculpe"** or **"Lamentamos"** — both valid depending on severity

---

## CTA Grammar

- Use **infinitive** for buttons: "Comprar", "Enviar" 
- Do NOT use imperative: "Compre", "Envie" 
- Base rule is the same as es locales — infinitive only

---

## Number & Currency Formats

| Format | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Currency | reais | R$1.500,25 |
| Thousands separator | **Period** (opposite of es_MX) | 1.000 |
| Decimal separator | **Comma** (opposite of es_MX) | 1.500,25 |
| Tax ID | CPF | 000.000.000-00 |
| Zip code | CEP | 01310-100 |

Number formatting is the **reverse** of es_MX — always verify before publishing.

## Grammar Notes

- **App**: MASC in pt ("**o** app") — opposite of es ("la app")
- **Internet**: lowercase — "internet" (opposite of es "Internet")
- **Bitso**: FEM — "a Bitso Transfer"
- **Pix**: Always "Pix" (capital P, lowercase ix) — as determined by the Central Bank of Brazil

---

## Future Tense

Use the "ir+infinitive" structure or the present tense.

| Structure | Example |
|---|---|
| ir+infinitive (preferred) | Seus fundos vão estar disponíveis em breve. |
| Present tense (informal) | Seus fundos ficam disponíveis em breve. |

---

## Verb Rules

- **Gerund in titles**: Avoid. Use infinitive instead. "Como criar sua conta na Bitso" / "Criando sua conta na Bitso"
- **Gerund post-action**: Avoid for sequential actions. "Você criou sua conta e uma carteira foi gerada automaticamente." / "Você criou sua conta, gerando uma carteira automaticamente."

---

## Time Formatting

- am/pm: not standard in PT-BR — prefer 24h format or "da manhã / da tarde / da noite"
- Hours symbol: "h" (same as all locales) — "24 h", "2 h"
- Minutes: "min" (no period)
- Seconds: "s" (SI standard, no period)

---

## Accessibility

- Toggle controls: **Ativar / Desativar** — never "Habilitar / Desabilitar"
- Sensory verbs: avoid "ver" — use "revisar" as neutral alternative

---

## Bitso+ / Earn Features (pt_BR)
- Use "fazer suas criptos renderem"
- Avoid: interest, savings, returns
