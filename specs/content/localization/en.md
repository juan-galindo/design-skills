# Locale: EN-US — American English

Sources: glossary.md, feel-framework.md, cta-guidelines.md, help-center.md

---

## Register

- **Pronoun**: "you"
- **Tone**: Casual American English. Direct, clear, approachable.
- **Contractions**: Encouraged — "you're", "we'll", "it's" sound more human than the full forms.
- **Spelling**: American English — "analyze" not "analyse", "color" not "colour"

---

## Verb Conjugations

| Form | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| CTAs / buttons | **Base verb form** (imperative) | "Buy", "Send", "Continue" / "To buy" |
| Body instructions | Direct, active voice | "Go to your Profile", "You can increase your limits" |
| Educational content | Direct questions | "Did you know you can schedule purchases?" |
| Success states | Warm but understated | "Done! You bought…" |

### Help Center Examples
- SP-MX "Ve a tu Perfil" → EN "**Go to** your Profile"
- SP-MX "Puedes aumentar tus límites" → EN "**You can** increase your limits"
- SP-MX "Haz clic en Actividad" → EN "**Click** on Activity" (web) / "**Tap** Activity" (mobile)

## FEEL Tone Patterns

| Tone | Pattern |
|---|---|
| Functional | Imperative, concise. "Enter the amount." |
| Emotional | Warm but understated: "Done! You bought…" |
| Educational | Direct questions: "Did you know you can…?" |

---

## Error Messages

- Shared accountability: **"Something went wrong"** or **"We ran into an issue"**
- Apology: **"We're sorry"** for serious errors / **"Sorry"** for minor friction

---

## CTA Grammar

- Use **base verb form** (imperative): "Buy", "Send", "Continue" 
- Do NOT use infinitive with "to": "To buy" 
- This is the only locale where imperative form is correct for buttons
- Capitalize first word only: "Buy crypto" / "Buy Crypto"

## Grammar Notes

- **Internet**: lowercase — "internet"
- **App**: neutral gender — "the app"
- **Bitso**: no article needed in English
- Avoid EM dash overuse (common mistake when translating from Spanish)
- Do NOT use emojis in product copy

---

## Number Formats

- Currency: $1,500.00 USD
- Thousands: comma → $1,500
- Decimal: period → $1,500.50
- Percentages: 12.5% (no space)
- Dates: March 14, 2025 (text) or 03/14/2025 (numeric — MM/DD/YYYY, American format)

---

## Future Tense

Use "will" + verb. Use the contracted form when the subject is a pronoun.

| Subject | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| Pronoun | Do: You'll receive a confirmation email. | Don't: You will receive a confirmation email. |
| Noun | Do: Bitso will send you a confirmation email. | Don't: Bitso'll send you a confirmation email. |

## Accessibility

- Toggle controls: **Turn on / Turn off** — never "Enable / Disable"
- Sensory verbs: avoid "see/watch" — use "review" as neutral alternative
- Emojis: never in product copy; marketing only
