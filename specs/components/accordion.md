## What it is

The Accordion vertically stacks a list of headers that reveal or hide associated sections of content. Each item consists of a heading, an expandable icon, and an accordion panel.

## When to use

- To group related information under a shared heading.
- To shorten long pages and reduce scrolling when content is not crucial to read in full.

## When NOT to use

- When there is only one group to expand — place that content directly on the screen instead of hiding it.

## Spacing

Items must be placed one after the other with **no spacing between them**.

### Clickable area

The entire header row (heading + icon) is the tap target — not just the icon.
