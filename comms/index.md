## Writing principles

All formats follow the same two rules:

**1. Action titles (McKinsey style)**
The title is the takeaway — not the topic.
Write an assertion, not a label. Answer "so what?" before the reader has to ask.

**2. Minto Pyramid (SCR)**
Lead with the conclusion. Support it with key points. Evidence comes last.
- **Situation** — context everyone agrees on
- **Complication** — what changed or went wrong
- **Resolution** — the recommended action (this is your title)
