# Leadership Update — Template

**Format:** Written doc (Confluence / Notion) or slides
**Audience:** Directors, PMs, cross-functional leads
**Cadence:** Bi-weekly or on demand
**Owner:** Design lead

> See [writing principles](../index.md#writing-principles) before filling in.

---

## When to use written doc vs slides

| Use written doc | Use slides |
|---|---|
| Async update — no meeting | Sync presentation with discussion |
| Dense information, tables, links | Visual work to walk through |
| Needs a permanent record | One-time communication |

---

## Structure

### Headline ← the action title

One sentence at the top. The single thing leadership needs to take away.
If they read nothing else, this sentence must be enough.

> "Design is on track for the Q2 release — one dependency on Eng needs resolution by MAY 20."
> Not: "Q2 Design Update."

---

### Status

| Item | Status | Owner | Due |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Stream or project] | 🟢 On track / 🟡 At risk / 🔴 Blocked | [Name] | [Date] |

---

### What's happening

Two or three paragraphs max. SCR structure:
- **Situation** — what the team is working on and why
- **Complication** — what is at risk or needs a decision
- **Resolution** — what you recommend or have already decided

No bullet walls. Write in full sentences.

---

### Decisions needed

What you need from leadership. Be explicit — one ask per line.

| Decision | Context | Needed by |
|---|---|---|
| [Specific ask] | [Why it's blocking] | [Date] |

No decisions needed → omit this section.

## Slide format

If using slides instead of a doc, map the structure to these slide types:

| Section | Slide type |
|---|---|
| Headline | Cover — action title only |
| Status | Content — table |
| What's happening | Content — statement (SCR in body) |
| Decisions needed | Content — call to action |
| Metrics | Content — pillars or table |
